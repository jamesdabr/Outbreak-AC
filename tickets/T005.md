## Acceptance criteria

2. Forged target/hit claims, impossible cadence, invalid ammo, duplicate actions and invalid timestamps do not award damage.
3. Both clients observe consistent movement/combat outcomes under the approved network profiles.
4. Outbreak permits friendly damage without inherited team-kill/friendly-fire punishment.
5. Protocol isolation and source ownership match the approved addendum.
