## Required outcome

Implement server-owned down/crawl/revive/elimination and fixed-match squad identities sufficient for universal rescue and restricted spectating.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Any living survivor can revive a downed survivor, including an opponent, without changing squad or damage permissions.
2. Bleed-out, repeated downs, downed damage, interruption, recovery lock and one-helper arbitration match frozen parameters.
3. Full-squad downing leaves individual rescue windows active; elimination is final.
4. Only eligible present alive/downed squad actors can be spectated; no unrelated/free-camera information leaks.
5. Disconnect, observer join and concurrent helper cases produce consistent server/client states.

## Out of scope

No team-only revive substitution, local-only acceptance, respawning eliminated players, party-service integration or contract balance changes.
