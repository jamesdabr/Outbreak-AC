## Acceptance evidence

For every ticket criterion, state PASS, FAIL or NOT RUN and provide commands, results and evidence paths. Include OS/toolchain/hardware for build or performance claims.

## Implementation status

Distinguish implemented behavior, simulations/test doubles and incomplete work. Identify limitations without describing them as accepted exceptions.

## Integration and questions

List interfaces touched, new dependencies/assets, unresolved questions and exact reproduction steps. Do not change authoritative ticket status; the lead reviews and accepts or returns this handoff.
