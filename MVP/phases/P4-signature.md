# P4 Eight-player signature MVP

Revision: 0.1. Contract status: DRAFT. Phase state: PLANNED. Authority: owner and coordinating lead only.

## Entry conditions

P3 ACCEPTED; eight-player scope approved in D001; P4 AI/Director parameters frozen.

## Required deliverable

Raise human client capacity to eight, use four extraction seats, add one Predator commanding existing walkers through front/flank choices, and add Director-lite within fixed regional/population budgets.

## Acceptance gate

1. Eight human-controlled client slots remain distinct from up to 12 walkers plus one Predator; authoritative interaction state remains consistent.
2. Predator decisions use sight/noise/alerts and bounded memory; a repeatable scenario demonstrates both front and flank choices.
3. Every command has an audible and non-color-only visible cue; killing the Predator ends coordination without deleting ordinary walkers.
4. Director records seed/reasons and respects frozen spawn visibility, travel, population and recovery constraints; requests cannot conjure unlimited infected.
5. Extraction seat count/deadline stay fixed and announced; Director never introduces an uncontracted crisis or skill-based squad punishment.

## Exclusions

No runners/brutes, multiple Predators, full-town map expansion, extra extract routes or public matchmaking.

## Tickets and handoff

Mandatory tickets: T011. Each requires an individual RELEASED assignment and write allowlist. Evidence goes in `notes/handoffs/`. Questions go in `notes/questions/`. Only the lead may accept this phase, revise its criteria or release the next phase.
